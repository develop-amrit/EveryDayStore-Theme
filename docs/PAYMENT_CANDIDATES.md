# UAE payment-provider shortlist — approval required

Theme code does not select a provider, collect payment details or contain payment keys. Verify the current list in **Settings > Payments**, the legal entity's UAE eligibility, grocery/category acceptance, AED settlement, fees, wallets, 3DS, refunds and support terms before choosing.

- **Telr / TelrOnsite:** Telr documents offsite and onsite Shopify payment apps. Its guide gives Settings > Payments > Add payment methods > Search by provider > Telr. UAE service and merchant onboarding still need approval. [Official Shopify integration guide](https://docs.telr.com/reference/shopify-integration-guide).
- **PayTabs:** documents a Shopify integration; available payment methods depend on region and Shopify payment-app restrictions. Confirm the exact enabled UAE methods rather than assuming a wallet is included. [Official Shopify payment-method documentation](https://support.paytabs.com/en/support/solutions/articles/60000760093-1-5-shopify-plugin-supported-payment-methods).
- **Checkout.com:** documents Shopify onsite/offsite integrations; suitability and merchant onboarding must be confirmed. Do not infer UAE Shop Pay availability from generic Shopify integration support. [Official Shopify integration scope documentation](https://support.checkout.com/hc/en-us/articles/29751022032786-Which-API-key-scopes-do-I-need-to-configure-my-Shopify-store-to-use-the-Payments-API).
- **BNPL:** Tabby and Tamara are candidates subject to provider/merchant/category approval, not promises of checkout availability. Keep theme instalment messaging off until enabled and approved. [Tabby integration](https://tabby.ai/en-AE/help-business/selling-with-tabby/integrating-with-tabby), [Tamara Shopify integration](https://docs.tamara.co/docs/shopify).

COD is a Shopify manual payment method (Settings > Payments > Manual payment methods > Cash on Delivery), not a payment-collection feature implemented by the theme. Dynamic payment icons reflect shop.enabled_payment_types. Actual card/bank offers are provider/discount configuration, with no fabricated discount in the storefront. Sources checked 2026-10-20; nothing was installed or activated.
